Apple Music

In Apple Music for Windows, press Control+Alt+Down Arrow to suggest less of the song or album containing keyboard focus.

Press Control+Alt+Up Arrow to favorite that item instead, or Control+Alt+Shift+Up Arrow to remove it from favorites. Removal only unchecks Favourite or uses an explicit removal command, and never adds a favorite. All three commands use the current-song route when focus is in player controls. NVDA does not read the menu or your item again when focus returns; you hear only the result.

Using the player

A focused Action/More button is used directly. Apple Music exposes its player More button to NVDA as “Action.” From other player controls, the add-on looks for an Action/More button grouped with transport controls. If it cannot identify one, it sends Control+L to reveal the current song. A newly selected song is focused before opening its context menu. The requested command is invoked by its accessible name. It attempts to restore your original focus afterward. Keep focus in place until you hear the result. If neither route exposes a target, the operation stops.

Moving between sections

Press F6 for the next section and Shift+F6 for the previous section. The order is Search, Sidebar, Player, Main content, Queue, Lyrics, then back to the first available section. Hidden sections are skipped. The last control left with F6 is remembered for each section. Use Tab and arrow keys within sections. Switching sections changes focus without activating controls.

Known and remembered controls are queried directly for faster F6 navigation. Closed Queue and Lyrics panels are skipped from their toggle state, avoiding a full playlist scan before Search. Fallback discovery remains available when Apple Music does not expose a reliable panel state.

Control+1 through Control+3 open Home, New, or Radio and leave you on the first item of the page, even when that page is already open. Control+4 and Control+5 open the Library or Playlists list. Control+6 opens Settings through the account menu. Control+S opens search and leaves you in the search field, ready to type. Any previous query is selected, so typing replaces it.

Cards whose titles exist only in artwork, such as Radio's On Air Now stations, are reported by Apple Music with internal names such as AMP.Services.CommonModels.LiveRadioGridLockup. The add-on reads the real title, such as Apple Music Hits, from the page data Apple Music caches on your computer, matched by section and position. If that data is missing or does not match the page, it falls back to the card's artists, or Live radio station.

Close menus and dialogs before switching sections. Detection uses English accessible names and roles. Queue and Lyrics need named accessible containers; unsupported controls are grouped into Main content.

Reading Home cards

Home grid cards include titles and metadata exposed inside the card, followed by context such as Made for You or New Release 2026. Duplicate artwork and text labels are read once. Accessible text clipped below the artwork is included. If no additional text is exposed, the original name is retained.

Personalized cards with an artist subtitle and no exposed title read Made for You, featuring the artists. The add-on cannot recover titles from unlabeled artwork. Decorative icon-font characters are omitted.

Repeated Home grouping names are omitted when a nearer group or the focused control already has the same name. Distinct section names remain. Corrected card names are available to speech and braille.

Album, radio show, and playlist tracks

A newly exposed track list receives focus after navigation. The add-on scrolls the track into view and remembers the list so subsequent focus moves are respected. F6 prefers tracks over album and playlist header controls. The selected row may be used instead of the first track.

Press Enter or Numpad Enter on a track row to play it. The add-on double-clicks a plain text cell of the row, such as the title, and then puts the mouse pointer back. It only clicks when Windows confirms that point belongs to that cell, so links, buttons and anything covering the row are never clicked. Otherwise it scrolls the row into view, opens its own More button when available, and otherwise tries Shift+F10. It selects Play or Play followed by the quoted song title. Play Next and Play Last are never selected. Radio shows presented as album tracks, such as Find Your Harmony episodes, use the same behavior.

Enter on a track's More button opens the menu normally. Other child controls keep their native action. Moving focus before the menu opens cancels playback, and menus arriving after the playback timeout are not acted on.

Press Home or End on a track row to move to the first or last track. The add-on waits briefly for Apple Music's virtualized list to expose the final track name before NVDA announces it.

Track recognition uses English numbered album names such as Track 1 and playlist rows ending with a duration, such as 3 minutes, 49 seconds. Tracks below the visible header are scrolled into view. Large pages with over 1,000 controls are supported. Pages that reuse a list without a focus event and unexposed virtual rows can require manual navigation. Moving focus elsewhere cancels a pending automatic move.

Feedback

NVDA announces “Suggest less” when the accessibility action succeeds. This does not independently verify that Apple's server saved the preference. If the menu contains Undo Suggest Less, NVDA says “Already set to suggest less” and leaves the preference unchanged. Unavailable actions produce an explanation.

The Favorite command announces “Added to favorites” after the accessibility action. If it finds a removal command or a checked Favorite item, it says “Already a favorite” without removing the favorite. American and British English menu names are supported.

Checkable menu items use Toggle only when their current state is explicitly off. Already checked or indeterminate items are never toggled. Invoke is used for commands that expose it without Toggle.

Changing the shortcut

With Apple Music active, open NVDA Preferences, Input gestures, and find the command under Apple Music. The shortcut is active only in AppleMusic.exe.

Requirements and limitations

This version targets NVDA 2025.1 through 2027.1 and English Apple Music menu labels. No AutoHotkey or external UI Automation library is needed. Version 0.4.9 passes 151 simulated-control tests. Live NVDA MCP testing on NVDA 2027.1 confirmed settled Home and End announcements in a 229-track playlist; Page Up and Page Down also matched refreshed focus reports. Fresh braille testing, full album transitions, and open Queue/Lyrics cycling still need testing.

Preference commands need a focused UIA row/card, a recognizable player More button, or a song newly focused or selected by Control+L. An old selection is never assumed to be the current song. Unsupported layouts may require manual navigation. Multiple selections reported by UIA are refused. Changing applications cancels pending work. A disappeared control cannot have focus restored.

Updating

Version 0.4.2 fixes playback from track rows by using the track's own More button when exposed. Enter on More keeps its normal action. Delayed playback attempts are cancelled when focus moves or the menu timeout expires.

Version 0.4.0 adds track-list focus and Enter playback. It recognizes the player's Repeat and Queue controls by their stable identifiers, keeping them out of Main content. It recognizes sidebar list items using the WinUI NavigationViewItem class and Sidebar automation IDs, so Home is no longer labelled Main content. Click to search and player audio-quality controls are also classified correctly. Rapid F6 and Shift+F6 presses share one scan; the latest direction wins. Queue's Playing Next and History tabs are recognized. Queue and Lyrics content appear as separate sections when their named accessible panels are open. F6 does not automatically open closed panels. Install this package over the earlier version and restart NVDA.
