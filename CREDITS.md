# Credits

This is an NVDA add-on for Apple Music on Windows. Apple Music's own keyboard navigation is slow and its Home cards announce badly with a screen reader. This add-on makes navigation faster and clearer, and reaches Favorite and Suggest Less with one key.

Apple Music is Apple's. NVDA is NV Access's. This add-on only adds the shortcuts and announcements between them.

Questions and bug reports are welcome in the [SerrebiProjects Telegram group](https://t.me/SerrebiProjects). More serrebi projects: [projects.serrebiradio.com](https://projects.serrebiradio.com).
